# Full-text and PDF-to-Markdown Policy

1. Record DOI/PMID/title, source URL, access route, license/authorization status, download date, checksum, and parser used.
2. Download only from lawful open-access, publisher/author, user-supplied, or already-authorized institutional routes.
3. Keep PDFs and full parsed text under ignored `fulltext-local/` unless redistribution rights are verified.
4. Use the globally configured `llamaparse` MCP for PDF-to-Markdown when useful; never persist its credential.
5. Compare parser output with the source PDF for tables, dosage numbers, confidence intervals, and recommendation wording.
6. Commit evidence extraction, page/section locators, and short compliant quotations—not a copyrighted full-text surrogate.
