### Key counter-bias findings to carry into synthesis (sponsor-bias guardrail — do not omit)

- Soliman/NeuPSIG 2025: α2δ-ligands (gabapentin+pregabalin pooled) NNT 8.9 (95% CI 7.4–11.10) is numerically worse than TCAs (NNT 4.6) and SNRIs (NNT 7.4), despite equal "strong, first-line" guideline grading across all three classes. This is class-pooled/mixed-etiology (`INDIRECT EVIDENCE` for pregabalin/DPN specifically), not a pregabalin-only or DPN-only estimate.
- COMBO-DN (T5): combination (duloxetine+pregabalin) was **not** significantly superior to high-dose monotherapy for the primary outcome (P=0.370); an uncorrected exploratory analysis favored duloxetine 60mg over pregabalin 300mg (P<0.001) — must not be overstated as a proven duloxetine-superiority finding, but also must not be omitted as a counter-balance to pregabalin-favorable framing.
- OPTION-DM (T6): all three pathways (amitriptyline-based, pregabalin-based, duloxetine-based) showed similar efficacy at week 16 (no pathway comparison reached significance) — no pregabalin-pathway superiority over amitriptyline- or duloxetine-based regimens.
- Combination therapy evidence (T2, T5, T6) is consistently modest/uncertain (GRADE low-certainty and/or non-significant primary outcomes), not proven synergy.

## Challenge-round guardrails (Decisions 2026-09-03-10, -11, -12 — mandatory for Wave 3 synthesis)

- T1 (Freeman 2008) is placebo-only; never implies relative superiority — always pair with T5/T6's null head-to-head findings.
- T3 (Soliman/NeuPSIG) NNT/NNH must always carry: "α2δ-ligands (pooling gabapentin and pregabalin; not a pregabalin-only estimate)."
- T2 (Frontiers 2026 combo SR) fixed phrasing: "Very limited, low-certainty evidence (GRADE low, 3 RCTs, 471 patients, only 2 poolable for the primary endpoint) suggests pregabalin+duloxetine combination may reduce short-term pain more than monotherapy; safety outcomes remain uncertain due to few trials and imprecision — not yet a robust basis for a routine combination recommendation."
- T5/T6 non-significant/exploratory findings must not be overcorrected into "pregabalin proven inferior" — correct framing: "head-to-head RCTs found no significant difference among pregabalin-, duloxetine-, and amitriptyline-based regimens."
- ADA/AAN pooled-class grading: pregabalin may be "one option within" the gabapentinoid class, never the grammatical subject of "is graded/recommended at Level A" without the class qualifier in the same sentence. 2011 AAN's pregabalin-specific Level A must always be cited as historical/superseded if used at all.
- Atmaca 2024 (G5) content requires an explicit "(expert opinion, not a formal consensus)" tag wherever quoted; its ALA claim and named point-of-care devices are unvalidated/expert-opinion tier, not guideline-endorsed.
- Respiratory-depression framing: state the "Warnings and Precautions, not Boxed Warning" classification and the "serious, life-threatening, or fatal" consequence in the same sentence, consequence last — never let the classification stand alone as implied reassurance. Never cite only the reassuring oxycodone/lorazepam/ethanol PD-interaction finding without the broader case-report/postmarketing warning in the same breath (flagged as a citation-fidelity check for the auditor, not just style).
- Compounded-risk callout (elderly + reduced renal function + concurrent CNS-depressant/diuretic/thiazolidinedione use) belongs in synthesis labeled `EXPERT INTERPRETATION`, not `DIRECT EVIDENCE`.
- Documentation-depth-asymmetry guardrail: pregabalin-safety's exhaustive harm documentation reflects scope assignment, not a finding of comparatively worse safety — use the guardrail sentence verbatim or adapt: "This depth of harm documentation reflects this role's dedicated verification scope, not a finding that pregabalin's overall safety profile is worse than other guideline-supported classes; readers should weigh it alongside the comparator harm profiles..." Respiratory depression with CNS depressants is class-wide (gabapentin carries an equivalent warning), not pregabalin-unique.
